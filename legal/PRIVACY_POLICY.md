# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **Hooko** ("Hooko," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Hooko mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Hooko is operated from **Ukraine**. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Account information:** Hooko does **not** require you to create a traditional account. When you first open the app, an **anonymous user identity** is automatically created on our backend — no email, name, password, or social login is collected at this time. We may add optional sign-in (such as Sign in with Apple) in a future version; this Policy will be updated if and when that happens.
- **Videos and media you upload:** short-form video files you submit for AI analysis. These are processed to generate hook scores, transcripts, hashtags, and rewrite suggestions. **Uploaded videos are automatically deleted from our servers immediately after the analysis completes** (see Section 4 and Section 5).
- **Communications:** messages you send us through support channels (e.g., email).

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & technical information:** device model, operating system version, app version, language, and time zone.
- **Persistent user identifier:** we use a stable user identifier provided by our subscription management provider to track your subscription entitlements and daily usage limits. On iOS this identifier is tied to your Apple ID and is **persistent across reinstalls and devices signed into the same Apple ID**, so your daily limits and subscription status follow your Apple ID even after uninstalling and reinstalling the app.
- **Usage data:** basic in-app events needed to operate the Service, such as the number of analyses you have run today (used to enforce daily limits).
- **Subscription & purchase data:** subscription status, plan tier, and renewal status — processed through our subscription management provider and the Apple App Store.

We do **not** currently use third-party crash reporting, analytics, or advertising SDKs. If we add any in the future, we will update this Policy.

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Payment processors** (Apple App Store and our subscription management provider): we receive subscription status and entitlements but **do not receive your full payment card details**.

### 1.4 Server Logs
Like virtually all internet-facing services, our backend providers generate **standard server logs** when your device communicates with them. These logs may briefly contain technical metadata such as your **IP address**, request timestamp, and HTTP status. We do **not** actively collect, profile, or sell this data; it exists solely for security, abuse prevention, and debugging, and is retained by our providers according to their standard log retention policies. We do not link these logs to your analysis content.

We do **not** knowingly collect precise location, contacts, health data, or biometric identifiers.

## 4. AI Processing of Your Videos

When you submit a video for analysis:
- The video is temporarily uploaded to our cloud storage so that our backend can process it.
- The video is processed by a third-party AI provider to extract a transcript and generate hook, audio, and visual scoring.
- We instruct our AI providers not to use your data for their own model training where such controls are available. We do not use your video content to train our own models.
- **As soon as the analysis is complete, your video file is automatically deleted from our storage by our backend.** If the server-side deletion fails for any reason, the app also performs a client-side fallback deletion. We do not keep a copy of your video after the analysis result is returned.
- Generated outputs (scores, transcripts, hashtags, rewrite suggestions, and a small thumbnail) are saved **only on your device** (in local app storage) as your personal analysis history. We do **not** store your analysis history on our servers.

## 5. Data Retention

- **Anonymous user record & subscription state** (stable user identifier, plan tier): retained on our servers while the identifier is in active use. Because the identifier is tied to your Apple ID, it can persist across reinstalls.
- **Uploaded videos:** stored in our cloud storage only for the brief period required to run the analysis (typically seconds to a couple of minutes). The video is **automatically deleted from our storage immediately after the analysis completes**, including in failure paths via a client-side fallback. We do **not** retain backups of your videos.
- **Analysis results (scores, transcripts, hashtags, rewrite suggestions, thumbnail):** stored **only on your device** in the app's local storage. They are **not** uploaded to or stored on our servers. They remain on your device until you delete them from the History screen, uninstall the app, or clear the app's data. We cannot recover this history if you delete it or lose your device.
- **Subscription & transaction records:** retained as required by law and for accounting (handled primarily by the Apple App Store and our subscription management provider).
- **Daily usage counters:** keyed by your stable user identifier plus the calendar date and retained while your identifier is active. They are used solely to enforce daily analysis limits.

You can request deletion of your account and associated server-side data at any time (see Section 9).
